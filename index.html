1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Bench Press Hero (the “App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use it. The App is a simple offline game. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask you for your name, e-mail address, or any other information that directly identifies you. The App displays advertising through Google AdMob, which is described in detail below.

By installing and using the App you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App saves your game progress locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. It includes:

  • your best result (repetitions) and total repetitions;
  • the number of games played;
  • your current and longest daily streak and the last day you played;
  • which in-game achievements you have unlocked.

This information is stored in the App’s private storage and is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data in your device settings.

3. Information Collected by Advertising

The App is free and supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To serve and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process data such as:

  • your device advertising identifier (Advertising ID) and similar identifiers;
  • your IP address, which may be used to infer an approximate (coarse) location;
  • device and app information (e.g. device model, operating system, language, app version);
  • information about your interactions with ads and general app usage related to advertising.

We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you personally. We do not sell any information ourselves. The App requests the following Android permissions, which are used solely for the purposes described here: internet access (INTERNET), network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) and the advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID).

4. How We Use Information

  • to display advertising that keeps the App free;
  • to remember your game progress on your device;
  • to measure ad performance and prevent fraud, as performed by our advertising provider.

5. Third-Party Advertising and Google

Advertising is delivered by Google. Google may use the data described above in accordance with its own privacy policies. We encourage you to review:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

6. Consent and Legal Basis

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before using your data for personalised advertising. Consent is collected through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP).

You can change or withdraw your advertising choices at any time using the “Privacy options” entry point on the App’s start screen. Where you have not consented, ads may still be shown on a non-personalised basis.

7. Your Choices and Opt-Out

  • Change your advertising consent via “Privacy options” in the App.
  • Reset or limit your Advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Uninstall the App or clear its data to delete the locally stored game progress.

8. Data Retention

Game progress is kept on your device until you delete it (by clearing the App’s data or uninstalling the App). Data processed by our advertising provider is retained according to Google’s own retention policies.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to keep the App safe. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at: maciejtrudnos@gmail.com.
